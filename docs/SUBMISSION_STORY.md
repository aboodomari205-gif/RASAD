# FIREPULSE submission story — current activated prototype

Updated 2026-11-14. This replaces the earlier 36-record pilot pitch. It is a presentation script, not official rules or an operational alert claim.

## 30-second explanation

MODIS and VIIRS have different footprints, acquisition times and confidence schemes, so their raw counts cannot be read as equivalent fire events. FIREPULSE organizes real Northern Jordan and Ajloun observations into one UTC calendar while preserving the source of every detection. Researchers can inspect original records, test possible cross-sensor correspondence, see how threshold choices change the result, and export the underlying evidence. Unknown coverage and unvalidated anomalies stay explicit.

## Concrete evidence, not universal calibration

Current catalogs contain 143 Ajloun and 6859 Northern Jordan detections. Ajloun is nested inside North. The focused Ajloun 2023 guide has 22 records: 2 MODIS Aqua and 20 VIIRS. July contains 18 records (2 MODIS / 16 VIIRS). At 1 km/60 minutes, the guide yields 12 candidate edges, 13 participating records and one connected group; at 1 km/7 minutes, 5/6/1. None of these graph counts is an independent confirmed-fire count.

The contribution is a reproducible source-aware calendar, interpretable correspondence and transparent evidence limits. It is not a validated sensor-neutral reconstruction. Exact official full-text acceptance remains unconfirmed; the retrieved official indexed summary and secondary reproduction are distinguished in OFFICIAL_REQUIREMENTS_TRACEABILITY.md.

## Three-minute judge path

Use [THREE_MINUTE_DEMO.md](THREE_MINUTE_DEMO.md). Start Mission is the only primary entry action; there is no Skip Intro button. Select Ajloun/2023 explicitly, then Calendar/July, inspect July 14 and download CSV or SVG. In Evidence Engine start the guide, compare 1 km/60 to 1 km/7, inspect original Aqua/S-NPP metadata, read the evidence limitations and export Research Brief. Optional Atlas playback visits delivered dates only; do not narrate the skipped days as verified zero activity.

Do not detour through every module. Existing season/DNA/sensor interfaces describe their bounded evidence; unavailable anomaly/calibration modules are deliberate scientific guardrails. The timed script is a rehearsal target, not a measured novice completion time.
